Foxstone Group Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 26 July 2026

Foxstone Group ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use our applications, games and digital services.

This Privacy Policy applies to all applications and games published by Foxstone Group, including but not limited to:

  • Foxstone Training
  • Forge Studios: Creative Director

This policy also applies to future applications and digital services published by Foxstone Group unless a separate Privacy Policy is provided.

Information We Collect

Depending on the application you use, we may collect:

  • Basic account information (where applicable)
  • Training progress and achievement data
  • Game progress and saved content
  • Device information
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information
  • Application usage statistics
  • Information you provide when contacting us for support

We do not intentionally collect more personal information than is necessary to operate and improve our products.

How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain our applications
  • Save your progress and settings
  • Improve performance and reliability
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Provide customer support
  • Develop new features and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

Third-Party Services

Our applications may use trusted third-party services to help operate and improve our products. These services may include cloud hosting, analytics, crash reporting, authentication and other technical infrastructure providers.

These providers only receive information necessary to perform their services and are responsible for handling data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Our products are intended for the audiences described within their respective app store listings. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws, including the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information where applicable
  • Object to certain processing activities

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and improve our applications.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us
